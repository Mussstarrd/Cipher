## How look-ahead bias was prevented

CFBD's rating endpoints are annual and return **end-of-season** values:
`/ratings/sp?year=2024&week=3` returns data identical to `/ratings/sp?year=2024`
(the `week` parameter is accepted and ignored), and the same holds for
`/ratings/fpi` and `/ratings/srs`. A season-Y rating therefore already knows the
result of the game being priced.

The rule applied without exception: **to price any game in season Y, only ratings
published before season Y started are used.** Concretely, for every week of Y:

- `sp+` <- season **Y-1 final** SP+
- `fpi` <- season **Y-1 final** FPI
- `talent` <- season Y talent composite (recruiting completes before the season, so
  this is preseason-knowable and not a result)

These are frozen for the whole season - a week 12 game is priced with exactly the
information available in August. The construction is visible in the output: the
2024 preseason-knowable top ten has Michigan 2nd and Florida State 10th, the teams
that went on to finish 8-5 and 2-10. A list contaminated by season-2024 results
could not produce that ordering.

Two ways this makes the backtest **pessimistic** rather than flattering:

1. From week 5 on, the live model may legitimately use in-season ratings; the
   backtest still prices week 12 off August data.
2. In weeks 1-4 the live model uses **current-season preseason SP+**, which folds in
   returning production, recruiting and portal movement. CFBD does not expose
   historical preseason SP+, so prior-year final SP+ is substituted - a weaker input.

This matters for interpreting the failure: the model did not merely fail to clear a
high bar, it failed while being measured generously against its own placebos.

## What this says about the model

Mean edge at entry is **16.2 points**. On the live week 1 slate the
same formula, fed current preseason ratings, disagrees with the market by a median
of 1.8 points. A 16-point average disagreement is not an edge; it is a rating that
has lost contact with the market. Regressing the market's closing number on the
model's gives a slope of **0.69** with a residual SD of **8.6 points** (live: 0.98
and 2.6). Prior-year-only ratings retain real signal but are far too noisy to bet,
and the fixed 2.0/2.5-point edge minimum - sized for a model with a ~2.5-point
residual - admits almost everything, qualifying 86% of priced games.

Per the plan: the gate failed, so the model gets restructured. No real money.
