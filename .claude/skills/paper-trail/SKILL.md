# Paper trail

Aiden photographs the thing that came home in his bag. That is the whole input.

The school-paper pile is a genuine source of household failure — forms surface the
morning they are due, or after. This closes that loop.

## Steps

1. **Read it.** Extract, and say which of these you could not find rather than
   guessing: what it is for, which child, the date of the event, the deadline to
   return it, whether money is involved and how much, and exactly what is required
   — information, a signature, payment, or all three.

2. **Decide which branch.**

### Branch A — it needs information and a signature

Rebuild it as a clean, fillable document that can go back to school.

- Pre-fill everything already known from `memory/reference.md` — child's full
  name, date of birth, address, both parents' phone numbers, emergency contact,
  doctor, allergies and medications. These are the same fields on every school
  form and re-typing them is most of the annoyance.
- Leave genuinely unknown fields blank and **list them** so Jeffery knows exactly
  what is left to do.
- Produce a `.docx` (see the `docx` skill) or a fillable `.pdf` (see `pdf`) —
  whichever suits the original. Keep the school's wording; this is a legible
  reproduction, not a rewrite.
- Send it to Jeffery with a one-line summary: what it is, when it is due, what it
  costs, what is still blank.

**Never sign on anyone's behalf.** Not a typed name, not a drawn signature, not
"on behalf of". Prepare the document; a parent signs it. This is not a
configurable rule.

### Branch B — it needs a wet signature on the original paper

Do not recreate it. Recreating a document that must be physically signed just
creates a second piece of paper to lose.

- Log it to `memory/open-loops.md` with the deadline and where the paper is.
- Surface it at **17:00 the day before it is due** — while there is still time to
  find a pen — and again at **07:00 on the day**, in the "before you leave"
  position.
- If the deadline passes untouched, log it to `misses.md`. A form that got missed
  despite reminders means the reminders were in the wrong place, and that is
  Hearth's problem to fix, not the family's.

## Always, both branches

- **File the photo** to Drive under the child's name and the school year, so the
  original is findable later. In standalone mode, keep it in the artifact's own
  storage instead.
- **Check whether it recurs.** Field trip forms, annual health updates, picture
  day, book fair, yearbook orders — most school paper is seasonal. If it looks
  annual, add it to `memory/rhythms.md` so next year it is expected rather than a
  surprise.
- **Capture the payment link** if there is one. Next year that link is most of the
  value. Never pay it — hand it over.
- **Tell Aiden it is handled**, in a sentence a nine-year-old would want to hear.
  He did the useful thing by photographing it; that habit is worth reinforcing.

## When there is no photo

A child saying "I need a form signed for the trip" is the same input with worse
data. Take it, ask only for the deadline, create the open loop, and reconcile it
when the paper appears.
