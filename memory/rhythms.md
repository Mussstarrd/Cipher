# Rhythms

Everything that recurs and has to **fire**. Distinct from `facts.md`: a fact is a
static truth, a rhythm has a clock attached.

This is the layer that makes Hearth worth having. Anyone can be told "soccer is
Tuesdays." Noticing in August that registration is about to open again, and
asking before the window closes, is the thing no calendar does.

## Format

```
### <name>
- who: <person>
- cadence: weekly Tue | monthly 1st | biweekly Fri | seasonal spring+fall | annual Sep
- lead: how far ahead to raise it
- last: YYYY-MM-DD — what happened
- next expected: YYYY-MM (confidence: told | observed once | observed N times)
- action: what Hearth does when it fires — always propose, never transact
- link: where it gets done
```

## Rules

1. **Never transact.** A rhythm firing produces a message and a link. Jeffery
   presses the button. This is not negotiable for anything involving money,
   registration, or a commitment to another person.
2. **Lead time is the whole point.** Firing on the day is useless. Registration
   windows close, classes fill, early-bird pricing expires. Default lead is three
   weeks for seasonal signups, one week for monthly bills, one day for weekly
   classes.
3. **Record the link when it happens.** The first time Jeffery signs Aiden up for
   soccer, capture where he did it. Next season that link is most of the value.
4. **Cash-flow timing beats amounts.** For bills, what matters is whether the
   debit lands before or after a paycheck. Track the ordering, flag the collisions.
5. **Decay unconfirmed rhythms.** If a rhythm was expected to fire and nothing
   happened, and nobody mentioned it, lower its confidence rather than repeating
   the reminder forever. Log it to `misses.md`.
