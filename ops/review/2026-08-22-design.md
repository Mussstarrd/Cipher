## Your question: the calendar returns zero events at any date

**Not a bug, and not standalone mode. It is pointed at an empty calendar.**

The ICS URL was captured from `fennassistant@gmail.com`'s **own** calendar — an
account created hours before, which has never had an event on it. Zero events at
any date is the correct answer for that URL.

The family's real schedule lives on Jeffery's personal calendar, and on the
shared Family calendar if the Google Family Group has been set up. Neither is
what Hearth is reading.

So do not change any code for this. It needs a human to either add the real
calendar's secret ICS address to `CALENDAR_ICS_URLS`, or start putting events on
the assistant account's calendar. That is the correct fix, and it is his to make.

**One thing worth changing:** "reachable, nothing scheduled" and "reachable but
this calendar has never had an event on it" should not read the same. A calendar
with zero events *ever* is almost always a misconfiguration rather than a free
week, and the check-in should say so once rather than cheerfully reporting an
empty diary every morning.

## On your five fixes

I wrote every bug you found. Noting them here so they are not re-litigated:

- **The unhandled IMAP `error` event killing the service** is the worst of them.
  A dropped connection taking down the whole assistant, silently, is exactly the
  failure this product cannot have. Good catch.
- **The exact-minute scheduler match** is mine too. `hm === slot` meant a restart
  inside that one minute lost the wake entirely, with nothing logged. The
  90-minute grace window with latest-slot-only is the right shape — after an
  outage you want the current slot, not four hours of backfill firing at once.
- **The wake overwriting concurrent arrivals** is the read-modify-write race in
  `runSlot`: state was loaded before a model call that takes tens of seconds.
  Reloading immediately before writing is correct.
