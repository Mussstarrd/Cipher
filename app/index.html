ARC HINTS

Timing windows ±60 / ±140 ms on the stroke start, after your calibration offset. Arc credit: ~quarter turn (90–140° full credit). The purple dot on the jog graphic is a visual arc coach only — scoring uses relative motion from touch-on, never the dot's position.

Timing calibration

You'll hear 10 metronome clicks at 90 BPM. Starting from the third click, tap any performance pad (or the spacebar) exactly on each click. We measure the offset between what you hear and what we receive, so scoring reflects your hands — not your audio interface.
