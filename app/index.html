ARC HINTS

Scored on the moment your stroke STARTS vs the arrow head crossing the line (±60 ms perfect / ±140 ms good, after your calibration offset). Arc size ~1/8 turn is ideal; anything 20–130° earns full credit, outside that you'll see SHORT/LONG ARC hints. A stroke that stalls >160 ms mid-arc counts as finished — snap, stop, wait. The purple dot is a visual arc coach only, never scored.

Timing calibration

You'll hear 10 metronome clicks at 90 BPM. Starting from the third click, tap any performance pad (or the spacebar) exactly on each click. We measure the offset between what you hear and what we receive, so scoring reflects your hands — not your audio interface.
