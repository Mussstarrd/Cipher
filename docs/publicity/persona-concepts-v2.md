## What to do with them

1. Open ChatGPT or Gemini. **Attach nothing.**
2. Paste one concept. Regenerate three or four times. Repeat for the other two.
3. Send me the one or two you keep looking at. If none of them do it, say which parts were close and which were dead, and I write round three.

Once one is approved it becomes the character sheet: every clip, cover and avatar is made from that single image, and consistency comes from always starting from it, not from re-prompting.
